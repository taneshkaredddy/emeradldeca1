WHO WE ARE

What is DECA?

DECA is a student organization dedicated to preparing high school and college students for careers in marketing, finance, hospitality, and management.

Through competitive events, conferences, leadership opportunities, and hands-on experiences, members have opportunities to develop leadership, business, and career skills they need to succeed.
