What are the eligibility and course requirements to join Emerald High School DECA?

To become an official, registered member of the Emerald High School chapter of California DECA and be eligible to compete at conferences, students must meet the following criteria:

CTE Course Enrollment: Students must currently be enrolled in an approved Emerald High School CTE Business Pathway course—currently Integrated Marketing Communications (IMC) or Economics of Business Ownership (EBO). Courses must be taken at Emerald High School. Seniors who have completed and passed both IMC and EBO at Emerald High School may continue to participate.

School Status: Students must be currently enrolled at Emerald High School and in good academic and behavioral standing.

Membership Dues: Students must pay the annual membership dues by the chapter's designated deadline. These dues cover official registration with California DECA and National DECA.

What is the expected time commitment for students who join DECA?

For D2 Members: The time commitment is minimal, as all events are optional. D2 members are primarily eligible for local events hosted by Emerald High DECA.

For D1 Members: Most preparation and training takes place in class because DECA is a co-curricular program. Students attending a conference must participate in at least 1 pre-trip meeting and 2 conference training events, totaling approximately 3 hours.

Students are also expected to study and practice independently. The main DECA written project aligns with the fall semester class project.

Is there anything students should do or prepare before joining DECA?

No prior preparation is necessary. Emerald High DECA provides resources and training to members once they join.

Students who want to learn more beforehand can visit DECA.org or join the DECA Direct email list.

How can student members become chapter officers?

The officer application process typically takes place in April each year. Current D1 members will receive more information when applications open. D2 members are not eligible to apply.

The selection process includes:

  • A written application
  • A knowledge test
  • An interview

A student's participation, behavior, and achievement throughout their DECA membership and class enrollment will also be considered.
