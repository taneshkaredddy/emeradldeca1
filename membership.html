Membership

Find your place in Emerald DECA and start building skills, connections, and experiences that can help prepare you for your future.

Students enrolled in an ROP Business Course are eligible to join Emerald DECA as competing members and participate in official DECA competitive events at NorCal, SCDC (State Career Development Conference), and ICDC (International Career Development Conference). Check out our divisions below to learn more!

D1

FULL COMPETITIVE MEMBERS

Members who are enrolled in a CTE class and are eligible to attend competitive events (NorCal, SCDC, ICDC).

  • Integrated Marketing Communications
  • Economics of Business Ownership
  • Sport and Entertainment Marketing
D2

CHAPTER MEMBERS

Members who are not enrolled in an ROP Business class cannot compete at official competitive events, but are able to participate in Emerald DECA chapter activities.

  • Emerald Mini-Con
