Officer Roles & Responsibilities

Our executive leadership structure is designed around DECA’s primary pillars. Here is an overview of how each role supports Emerald DECA members and operations.

Chapter President

Directs the executive board, sets annual goals, acts as the primary liaison to school administration, and oversees execution of all chapter initiatives.

VP of Leadership

Drives member engagement, runs team-building programs, manages chapter committees, and develops leadership training modules.

VP of Finance

Maintains chapter financial records, oversees fee collections, drafts budget proposals, and coordinates chapter fundraising initiatives.

VP of Marketing

Manages public relations, designs promotional graphics, runs official social media accounts.

VP & Director of Career Development

Lead all competitive event prep, coordinate practice exams and roleplays, organize study groups, and host prep workshops.

VP of Hospitality & Director of Records

Ensure smooth meeting logistics, coordinate social gatherings, manage conference details, and maintain official attendance and chapter records.
