Officer Roles & Responsibilities

Our executive leadership structure is designed around DECA’s primary pillars. Here is an overview of how each role supports Emerald DECA members and operations.

President

Establishes chapter vision, manages and organizes the executive board, and ensures each officer has the training, support, accountability, and resources needed to provide a strong DECA experience for members.

VP of Leadership

Leads non-competitive career and leadership activities, helping members develop leadership skills and creating opportunities for engagement beyond competitive events.

VP of Finance

Manages chapter budgeting, accounting, membership finances, and fundraising efforts to support Emerald DECA's activities and opportunities.

VP of Marketing

Leads member recruitment, manages chapter branding and promotions, and helps build Emerald DECA's presence throughout the school community.

VP of Career Development

Leads competitive event preparation, coordinates roleplay practice and exams, and supports members in developing the skills needed to succeed at DECA conferences.

VP of Hospitality

Creates a welcoming and engaging experience for members by supporting meetings, social activities, conferences, and other chapter experiences.

Director of Records

Takes meeting minutes and maintains organized records of chapter meetings, discussions, and important decisions.

Officer Roles & Responsibilities

Our executive leadership structure is designed around DECA’s primary pillars. Here is an overview of how each role supports Emerald DECA members and operations.

Chapter President

Directs the executive board, sets annual goals, acts as the primary liaison to school administration, and oversees execution of all chapter initiatives.

VP of Leadership

Drives member engagement, runs team-building programs, manages chapter committees, and develops leadership training modules.

VP of Finance

Maintains chapter financial records, oversees fee collections, drafts budget proposals, and coordinates chapter fundraising initiatives.

VP of Marketing

Manages public relations, designs promotional graphics, runs official social media accounts.

VP & Director of Career Development

Lead all competitive event prep, coordinate practice exams and roleplays, organize study groups, and host prep workshops.

VP of Hospitality & Director of Records

Ensure smooth meeting logistics, coordinate social gatherings, manage conference details, and maintain official attendance and chapter records.
