Conferences

NCDC

Northern District Career Development Conference. Connect with local chapters, practice roleplay events, and kickstart your competitive journey.

  • Compete in roleplay and writtens
  • Must be called to stage during closing ceremony or receive finalist pin to move to SCDC
  • There will be an option to appeal to also go to SCDC (NOT GUARANTEED)

SCDC

State Career Development Conference. Compete against top members across California for state titles and a spot at the international level.

  • Compete in roleplay and writtens
  • Required to attend AT LEAST 2 workshops
  • Must be called to stage during closing ceremony or receive finalist pin to move to ICDC:
    • Roleplays: Top 6
    • Writtens: Top 5
    • SMG & VBC Finalists

ICDC

International Career Development Conference. The final DECA conference bringing together over 20,000 members worldwide to compete and network.

  • Compete in the event that you qualify for
  • Network with international competitors
  • Must place top 3 in your respective events to win DECA GLASS
